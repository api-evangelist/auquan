ERM, the world's largest specialist sustainability consultancy, and Auquan, a leader in agentic AI for institutional finance, today announced a collaboration to deploy AI agents across sustainability workflows for financial institutions. ERM will leverage Auquan's Sustainability Agent to enhance the speed, scale, and depth of sustainability insights it delivers to clients navigating complex regulations and investment mandates.
